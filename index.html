Privacy policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Phaze Browser has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer of Phaze receives no data about what you browse; the only data that reaches the developer is crash reports, which you can turn off.

This policy applies to the Phaze Browser app for Android (package app.phaze.browser), developed by Tôn Tấn Phát.

Data kept on your device

Phaze keeps these only on your device, in storage other apps cannot read, and leaves them out of backups and device transfers:

  • browsing data kept by the web engine: history, cookies, site storage and cache;
  • your bookmarks;
  • the tabs you had open, so Phaze reopens them where you left off (private tabs are never saved, and their history, cookies and site data are deleted when they close);
  • the add-ons you added and their own data;
  • the searches you typed in normal tabs, offered again when you edit the address (Settings > Clear search history removes them);
  • the list of files you downloaded in normal tabs (the files stay in your Downloads folder), and the translation language models you downloaded;
  • your settings (theme, toolbar position, ad blocking, search suggestions, crash reports);
  • where you stopped in videos, so they resume there.

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Phaze, deletes all of it.

Connections Phaze makes

  • Websites you visit receive your requests directly, as with any browser. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Search: text you type in the address field that is not a web address is sent to Google Search. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Page translation: pages are translated on your device; their text is never sent anywhere. The first time you translate between two languages, Phaze downloads their language models from Mozilla (you are told the size first).
  • Add-ons: add-ons you choose come from Mozilla Add-ons (addons.mozilla.org); Phaze checks there for their updates once a day. An add-on can only do what its permissions, shown before you add it, allow, and it is covered by its own developer's privacy policy, not this one.
  • Ad blocking: when you turn on Settings > Block ads, Phaze downloads uBlock Origin from Mozilla Add-ons (addons.mozilla.org) and checks there for its updates once a day; uBlock Origin then downloads and updates its filter lists from the servers that publish them. These requests carry no information about the pages you visit. The filtering itself happens on your device.
  • Search suggestions: while you type in the address field, the text is sent to Google to get suggestions, without cookies or any identifier from Phaze. Web addresses with a path, a query or an @ are never sent this way. Turn it off in Settings > Search suggestions. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Safe Browsing: to warn you about phishing and malware, Phaze downloads lists of dangerous sites from Google Safe Browsing. When a page you open matches an entry, a short partial hash of its address (not the address itself) is sent to check it.
  • Crash reports: when Phaze crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service the developer uses to find and fix crashes: where in the app the crash happened, the app and Android versions, the device model, free memory and storage, the time, and a random identifier of this installation (not linked to you or your Google account). It never includes the pages you visit, what you type, your bookmarks or your files. Phaze also sends such a report, without crashing, when the web engine stops behind a page (whether the tab was on screen) and when a video fails in the Phaze player (the kind of error, the video format and the name of the decoder that failed). Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. Turn them off in Settings > Send crash reports. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Video player: when you play a video in the Phaze player, it fetches the video directly from the website, sending that site's cookies only when the video is on the same site as the page, and only the page's origin as referrer.
  • Web engine: the web engine Phaze is built on (GeckoView, by Mozilla) downloads tracking-protection lists and security and configuration updates from Mozilla's servers, in the background. These requests carry no information about the pages you visit.

Phaze connects to no other servers. Phaze does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone other than the services listed above.

Permissions

Internet and network state (to load pages), wake lock (to keep the screen on while a video plays), audio settings (to change the volume with a swipe in the player) and notifications (to show downloads; Phaze asks once and works without it). When websites ask for your camera, microphone, location or notifications, Phaze declines.

Files you save

Video screenshots (Pictures/Phaze) and downloads (Download) are saved only when you ask, to your device's shared storage.

Children

Phaze is not directed to children under 13.
