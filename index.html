Privacy policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Phaze Browser has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer of Phaze receives no data about what you browse; the only data that reaches the developer is crash reports, which you can turn off.

This policy applies to the Phaze Browser app for Android (package app.phaze.browser), developed by Tôn Tấn Phát.

Data kept on your device

Phaze keeps these only on your device, in storage other apps cannot read, and leaves them out of backups and device transfers:

  • browsing data kept by the web engine: history, cookies, site storage and cache;
  • your bookmarks;
  • the tabs you had open, so Phaze reopens them where you left off;
  • your settings (theme, toolbar position, crash reports);
  • where you stopped in videos, so they resume there.

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Phaze, deletes all of it.

Connections Phaze makes

  • Websites you visit receive your requests directly, as with any browser. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Search: text you type in the address field that is not a web address is sent to Google Search. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Safe Browsing: to warn you about phishing and malware, Phaze downloads lists of dangerous sites from Google Safe Browsing. When a page you open matches an entry, a short partial hash of its address (not the address itself) is sent to check it.
  • Crash reports: when Phaze crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service the developer uses to find and fix crashes: where in the app the crash happened, the app and Android versions, the device model, free memory and storage, the time, and a random identifier of this installation (not linked to you or your Google account). It never includes the pages you visit, what you type, your bookmarks or your files. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. Turn them off in Settings > Send crash reports. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Video player: when you play a video in the Phaze player, it fetches the video directly from the website, sending that site's cookies only when the video is on the same site as the page, and only the page's origin as referrer.

Phaze connects to no other servers. Phaze does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone other than the services listed above.

Permissions

Internet and network state (to load pages), wake lock (to keep the screen on while a video plays), audio settings (to change the volume with a swipe in the player) and notifications (to show downloads; Phaze asks once and works without it). When websites ask for your camera, microphone, location or notifications, Phaze declines.

Files you save

Video screenshots (Pictures/Phaze) and downloads (Download) are saved only when you ask, to your device's shared storage.

Children

Phaze is not directed to children under 13.
